# Changelog

All notable changes to **Strike It Right! Your Interactive Strike and Dip Laboratory** are documented here.
Made by @alisgneiss.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.8] - 2026-11-02
### Added
- Top switcher with separate pages: **Lab**, **Test yourself Quiz** and **Field notes**.
- "Open this problem in the Lab" button in the quiz.
- Direct links to pages with `#quiz` and `#notes`.
### Changed
- Quiz renamed to **Test yourself Quiz**, with the "board exam" wording removed.
- Mobile-friendly switcher (full-width row on small screens).

## [1.7] - 2026-11-01
### Added
- Attitude dashboard: six tiles with mini compass icons.
- Animation On/Off button (remembered between visits).
- New field note: "Sketch it in ten seconds".
- One-time tip bubble: "Want to convert azimuth ⇄ bearing?"
### Changed
- Wider layout for 16:9 desktop monitors.
- Diagram controls moved below the figure.
- Field notes redesigned as 12 cards with topic tags and key-formula strips.
- Bigger, more noticeable Azimuth ⇄ Bearing button.
- Given box redesigned as dashboard tiles with a highlighted Find strip.
### Fixed
- "True dip (δ)" label was displayed as "TRUE DIP (Δ)".

## [1.6] - 2026-10-31
### Added
- About 3 seconds of drawing animation on each step change, with a Replay button.
### Changed
- Section direction step 5 explains that the blue α line comes from the wanted apparent dip, and shows the OC calculation.

## [1.5] - 2026-10-31
### Added
- "My h = __ cm" box that converts every length into centimetres.
- Given box at the top of every guide.
- Step 1 shows how β is calculated (for example |80° − 35°| = 45°).
### Changed
- Graphical method guides rewritten in Do / Why format with exact protractor and set-square instructions.
- Correct depth-line construction for points A, P and C; "underground side" explained.
- Each guide states whether to count clockwise or counterclockwise.
- "Fold up" split into two steps: mark F′/B′, then join and read the angle.
- Dip input renamed to "True dip (δ)".
### Fixed
- Lines jumping or shrinking between steps.

## [1.4] - 2026-10-29
### Added
- Zoom and pan on diagrams (buttons, Ctrl + scroll, pinch, drag).
### Changed
- Dip direction shows the wrap past 360° (for example 410° − 360° = 050°).
- Explains which end of the section line the apparent dip plunges toward.

## [1.3] - 2026-10-27
### Added
- Floating Azimuth ⇄ Bearing converter with a mini compass and the conversion rule.
### Fixed
- Overlapping labels in diagrams; thinner highlight; clearer text outlines.

## [1.2] - 2026-10-27
### Added
- Night mode / Day mode button (remembered between visits).
- Scale bars on three-point, thickness and drillhole diagrams.

## [1.1] - 2026-10-27
### Added
- Trigonometry | Graphical method switch on every guide (fold-line constructions).
- Cartesian grid with N/S/E/W axes centred on point O.
### Fixed
- Thickness band stopped short in the cross-section.
- Quiz lesson 3D block was small and off-centre.

## [1.0] - 2026-10-27
### Added
- Attitude bar (any notation, right-hand rule), compass map view and rotatable 3D block view.
- Solvers: Apparent dip, True dip (one or two apparent dips), Three-point problem, Section direction, Thickness, Drillhole depth, Stereonet (Wulff/Schmidt).
- Formula legends and step-by-step guides with diagrams that build one piece at a time.
- Multiple-choice quiz: 10 random items across 7 topics, with guided lessons for missed items.
- Field notes with exam tips.
- Ridge-style design, Arial font, navy dark mode.
